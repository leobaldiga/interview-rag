## Output Format (follow exactly)

Tag format: <P_PRIMARY:S_SECONDARY:T:TERTIARY-TAG-TEXT>

Rules:
- Use underscores in primary and secondary codes: P_AOP, S_FBH, S_CMP, etc.
- Use colons between hierarchy levels: P_AOP:S_FBH:T:
- Tertiary tag text must be copied VERBATIM from the schema. Do not paraphrase or invent.
- Tags are self-closing — no separate closing tag.
- Tags go at the END of the complete speaker turn, after all spoken text.
  Never insert a tag mid-sentence or mid-turn.
- Multiple tags on the same turn are placed consecutively with a single space between them.
- If no schema tag fits a turn, do not insert any tag.
- Short backchannels and clarifying questions ("Mhm.", "Yeah.", "500?") 
  do not receive tags unless they contain substantive codeable content.
- Do not begin your output with any preamble or explanation.
- Do not add any text after the final speaker turn.
- Do not add summary lines, section headers, or end markers.
